      How this radar works

      Every figure comes from the sealed snapshot (DefiLlama protocols data, one frozen fetch). A protocol is called pre-token only because the snapshot records no token for it — the record may miss one. Implied values are the model's own claim about what a token would be worth if one were issued and the protocol's record stayed as it is; they predict neither whether nor when a token is issued, and they are not price targets.
